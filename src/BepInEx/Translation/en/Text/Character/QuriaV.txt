##Profile##
【魔法】前衛で、奇跡により魂の疵を癒す浄罪の戦乙女。味方を物理攻撃から堅牢に守り、継続ダメージを与える聖浄の光を敵に浴びせ、戦乙女の聖槍による天罰を下す。=[Magic] A Valkyrie of Absolution who stands on the front lines, healing the wounds of the soul through miracles. She steadfastly protects allies from physical attacks, bathes enemies in Sacred Purifying Light that inflicts continuous damage, and delivers divine punishment with her holy spear.
