##Profile##
【魔法】後衛で、天の星を瞳に映し、仲間と進む黒猫娘。敵全体を激烈に弱体化させながら、【千里真眼】状態時に未来を視通す能力で仲間をあらゆる脅威から遠ざける。=[Magic] A black cat girl in the back line who reflects the stars of the heavens in her eyes and advances with her allies. While heavily weakening all enemies, she uses her foresight in the [Clairvoyant Eye] state to protect her allies from all threats.
