##Profile##
【物理】前衛で、宙を舞うように戦う幽霊の守護騎士。ＵＢで味方の攻撃と速度支援を行い、【レイスボディ】で敵の目を逃れ、不意を突く一撃で永遠の眠りにつかせる。=[Physical] A ghostly knight who fights in the frontlines as if she were dancing in mid-air. Her UB buffs the attack and speed of her allies, and she uses her ability, [Wraith Body] to escape the eyes of the enemy, and attack where they least expect, sending them to an eternal sleep.
