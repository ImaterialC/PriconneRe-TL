##Profile##
【物理】中衛で恥じらいながらも激戦に飛び込む桃闘者。味方を【スパイクシール】で守りつつ、自身は物理防御力ダウンで敵を切り崩し、鍛え上げた桃尻を存分に振るう。=[Physical] A bashful peach fighter who leaps into fierce battles from the midline. While protecting allies with [Spike Seal], she breaks through enemies by lowering their P.DEF and fully unleashes her well-trained peach power.
