##Profile##
【物理】中衛で、過酷なお尻の世界を生き抜く桃闘者。攻防ダウンを併せ持つＵＢを矢継ぎ早に放ち、ステータスダウン効果で付与する【魅惑の矢】で敵を骨抜きにする。=[Physical] A peach fighter in the midline who survives the harsh world of peach combat. She rapidly unleashes UBs that inflict both offensive and defensive debuffs, and weakens enemies with [Enchanted Arrow] granted by status-down effects.
