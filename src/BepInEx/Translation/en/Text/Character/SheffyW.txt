##Profile##
【物理】中衛で兄妹の絆と不撓の心で覚醒した氷竜少女。個も群も薙ぎ払うＵＢと、【氷竜超克】状態時に発生する追撃状態で敵を咬断し、大切な人と歩む未来を切り拓く。=[Physical] A midline Ice Dragon girl awakened by the bond with her brother and her indomitable spirit. She sweeps away both lone foes and crowds with her UB, and while in [Ice Dragon Transcendence], follow-up attacks relentlessly tear through enemies, carving a path toward the future with those she holds dear.
